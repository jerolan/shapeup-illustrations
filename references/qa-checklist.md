# Inspect the bitmap

- Can the intended action or relationship be understood at article reading size?
- Does the image communicate the supplied idea without invented facts or misleading arrows?
- Is it drawn artwork throughout, with no screenshot content, screenshot overlays, or realistic product UI?
- Are the contours naturally hand-drawn, the forms simple, and the whitespace generous?
- If people appear, do posture and gesture carry the meaning while proportions stay consistent?
- Does each accent color identify something rather than decorate the page?
- Do the ink, background, and accent follow explicit requests before applicable DESIGN.md values and defaults? Are text and important marks readable against the chosen background?
- Is every intended label correct and readable, including accents? Is any unrequested text present?
- Are bubbles, labels, people, and important marks entirely inside the canvas?
- For a series, do colors, recurring objects, and characters retain their identity?
- For progression, can the viewer follow the same entities through ordered states and identify the visible change at each transition, even with captions covered?
- Does the shared frame remain stable, with branching, accumulation, stalls, or reversals matching the supplied account? Are historical positions distinguishable from separate items?
- Does each movement or completion mark have the intended meaning, without implying unsupported percentages, time measurements, or a finished outcome?
- For a guided explanation, does each visible edit correspond to the current explanatory beat, and does its brief message describe the implication rather than merely rename the drawing? Can an unchanged conclusion remain unchanged until the decisive step?
- For lists, can additions, edits, moves, cancellations, and completions be distinguished while unchanged entries remain recognizable?
- For a requested four-panel conversation, is there one 2×2 bitmap in the intended reading order, with correct in-panel dialogue, clear speakers, and a visible development of the shared idea?
- Does the actual output satisfy the requested background, format, and orientation?

Repair concrete failures with GPT. A denser diagram usually needs fewer elements or a short sequence; a confusing vignette needs a clearer action or reaction. Text defects need the exact corrected strings. Do not regenerate merely to chase unspecified aesthetic preferences.

Report only checks actually performed. An inaccessible bitmap is an unverified result, even when the generator reports success.
